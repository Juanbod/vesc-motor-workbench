# Original-motor comparison target

User objective: approach or exceed the original Dualsky performance. This is a development target, not a demonstrated property of the modified rotor.

Manufacturer reference: [ECO 3520C-V2 specification, pages 1 and 8](https://shop.dualsky.com/u_file/2604/file/4a71ea2906.pdf), accessed 2026-09-13. Identification matches the user-reported model and KV; the specimen label has not been independently checked.

- Original 510 KV variant: SKU 51672, 8 turns, 12 slots / 14 poles, 210 g, 5-6S LiPo.
- Published 15-second maxima: 48 A and 1150 W. These are not continuous ratings or permission to use the same currents on the modified motor.
- Published optimum-efficiency point in the 6S section: 9940 RPM, 21.49 V, 29.16 A, 626.6 W electrical, 548.8 W mechanical, 87.6% efficiency.

Comparison policy: progress toward approximately 10,000 RPM through qualified 500-RPM steps. Do not confuse reaching that speed unloaded with matching the published loaded point. KV times our observed 24.4 V gives an idealized original-motor no-load estimate of 12,444 RPM, NOT a mechanical speed rating or a prediction for this magnet-free motor.

The present bench measures encoder-derived speed and VESC DC consumption only. Shaft torque, shaft power, efficiency and continuous thermal rating remain unmeasured. Original 14 poles must not overwrite the experimental configuration's independently investigated ratio of two pole pairs. Never change protections solely to match a brochure figure.
